# Sprint 1 Retrospective

**Owned by:** Scrum Master

This file is completed at the end of week 2. Review what your team shipped, what went well, what went poorly, and one specific process change for Sprint 2.

## What Went Well

We worked as a team to achieve the final goal of the lab. We divided the work evenly as a team so each person was responsible for completing a task that was assigned to them. We implemented the divide-and-conquer method. We navigated through the different schedules of the teams to meet after class hours.

## What Could Be Better

As this is a new class, there were many new processes that we were testing as a team. We discovered many issues; for example, the VM running out of space and a firewall issue that we could not fix ourselves. It was a surprise, and while it was a good learning opportunity, running through some technical issues that definitely slowed the team down. 

## What We'll Do Differently Next Sprint

For the next sprint, we will change the roles in our team so everyone is able to experience a new role for each sprint. For example, Jakob was the Scrum master for the first sprint, and now the sprint’s 2 Scrum master will be Marcos.
